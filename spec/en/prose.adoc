= Prose
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

These rules make text quick to read and easy to translate.
The {abbr-fpsot-first} prose checker tests most of them.
The xref:../../docs/modules/en/pages/prose-checker.adoc[prose checker reference] maps each of its messages to a rule.

== Plain language

[[WRT-001]]
*WRT-001.* Text MUST use plain language.
Readers must find what they need, understand it the first time, and act on it.
These are the three principles of link:https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html[ISO 24495-1:2023].
A specification is for public servants, for development teams and for the public.
It cannot assume an expert reader.

[[WRT-002]]
*WRT-002.* A sentence SHOULD have 20 words or fewer.
Long sentences hide the main point and slow readers down.
They also translate badly.

[[WRT-003]]
*WRT-003.* A paragraph SHOULD have 6 sentences or fewer.
Short paragraphs let readers scan a page and find their place again.

== Sentences

[[WRT-004]]
*WRT-004.* Sentences SHOULD use the active voice.
The active voice names who acts.
A rule then says who must do what.

[[WRT-005]]
*WRT-005.* Text SHOULD use a verb rather than a noun made from that verb.
Write `configure the server` rather than `perform the configuration of the server`.
The verb is shorter and names the action.

[[WRT-006]]
*WRT-006.* Text SHOULD NOT use the vague words and filler phrases that the prose checker lists.
Words such as `various` or `in order to` add length without meaning.

[[WRT-007]]
*WRT-007.* Text SHOULD NOT join two clauses with a dash or a semicolon.
Two sentences are easier to read and to translate.

[[WRT-008]]
*WRT-008.* Text SHOULD NOT put needed information in parentheses.
Readers skip text in parentheses.
Put needed information in the sentence, or in a sentence of its own.

== Procedures

[[WRT-050]]
*WRT-050.* Step-by-step procedures MAY follow link:https://www.asd-ste100.org/[ASD-STE100], Simplified Technical English.
It limits each step to one instruction and uses a small set of words with one meaning each.
