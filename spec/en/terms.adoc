= Terms and abbreviations
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

All {abbr-fpsot-first} specifications share one term base.
This specification owns it, in the file `termbase/terms.yaml`.
Each entry holds the term in English and in French, with its full form and a short definition.

[[WRT-020]]
*WRT-020.* A defined term or an abbreviation MUST come from the term base.
A page MUST write it with the attribute of that entry: `{term-…}` for a term and `{abbr-…}` for an abbreviation.
The attribute links the term to the glossary.
It also marks an abbreviation up for screen readers and shows its full form when the pointer rests on it.

[[WRT-021]]
*WRT-021.* A page MUST spell an abbreviation out the first time it uses it, through the `{abbr-…-first}` attribute.
The pop-up full form does not work on every touch screen or with every screen reader.
The spelled-out form works for everyone.

A term that is missing from the term base goes in through a pull request to this specification.
The xref:../../docs/modules/en/pages/write-a-requirement.adoc[how-to guide] shows the steps.
