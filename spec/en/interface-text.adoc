= Interface text
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

These rules cover the words on screens: labels, help text and messages.

[[WRT-040]]
*WRT-040.* An error message MUST say what happened and what the reader can do next.
It MUST NOT blame the reader.
A reader who knows the next step can fix the problem alone.
Blame makes people give up on a public service.

[[WRT-041]]
*WRT-041.* Interface text SHOULD use the words of the reader, not the names inside the system.
Write "your application" rather than "case record".
People understand their own words without help.
