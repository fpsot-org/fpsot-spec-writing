= Language quality
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

{abbr-fpsot-first} publishes every text in US English and Canadian French.

[[WRT-030]]
*WRT-030.* English text MUST use US spelling.
The reference is the link:https://www.merriam-webster.com/[Merriam-Webster] dictionary.
Most standards bodies and open-source projects write in US English, so readers and tools expect it.

[[WRT-031]]
*WRT-031.* French text MUST follow the usage of the {abbr-oqlf-first}.
The references are its Grand dictionnaire terminologique and its Banque de dépannage linguistique.
Québec public bodies follow the same references.

[[WRT-032]]
*WRT-032.* French text SHOULD use gender-inclusive writing, as the {abbr-oqlf} describes it.
Prefer words that name a group or a role, such as "le personnel", over doubled forms.
French text MUST NOT use new gender-neutral words, such as "iel".
It MUST NOT use shortened doubled forms, such as "citoyen·ne·s", in running text.
Tables and forms may use shortened forms in parentheses when space is tight.
Inclusive text addresses every reader.

[[WRT-033]]
*WRT-033.* Machine translation MAY produce a first draft only.
A person MUST review the draft before it merges.
Machine translation misses terms and register.
A reviewed text keeps the quality of both languages equal.
