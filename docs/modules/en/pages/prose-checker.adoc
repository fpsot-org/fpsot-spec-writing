= Prose checker reference
:page-type: reference
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

The {abbr-fpsot-first} prose checker reads every page of a specification in its continuous integration.
This page maps each of its messages to the rule it checks.

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Message |Rule |What to do
|`N words` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-002[WRT-002] |Split the sentence in two.
|`paragraph has N sentences` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-003[WRT-003] |Split the paragraph, or cut a sentence.
|`passive?` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-004[WRT-004] |Name who acts, and make them the subject.
|`nominalization` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-005[WRT-005] |Use the verb the noun comes from.
|`vague word` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-006[WRT-006] |Cut the word, or say exactly what you mean.
|`dash or semicolon joins clauses` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-007[WRT-007] |Write two sentences.
|`parenthesis` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-008[WRT-008] |Move the text into the sentence, or cut it.
|`noun cluster` |xref:../../../../spec/en/prose.adoc#WRT-001[WRT-001] |Add the words that link the nouns, such as "of" or "for".
|===

The checker skips code, listings and tables.
It also skips attribute lines and quoted text.
In French it checks sentence and paragraph length only, because its word lists are English.
The list of vague words is in `rules/prose-lint.md`.

A finding is a prompt to look again, not a verdict.
The checker can flag a correct sentence, such as a passive that reads better.
Reviewers decide.
